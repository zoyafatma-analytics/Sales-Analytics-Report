# 📊 Sales Analytics Report | AtliQ Hardware

## 📌 Project Description
Sales Analytics Report is an Excel-based business analytics project developed for AtliQ Hardware to analyze customer sales performance, product trends, market performance, and revenue growth across multiple sales channels.

The project focuses on transforming raw sales datasets into meaningful business insights using ETL processes, data modeling, DAX measures, Pivot Tables, and interactive Excel dashboards. The analysis helps evaluate sales growth, target achievement, customer performance, and product-level profitability for better business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze customer and market sales performance across different years.
- Compare actual sales against target sales performance.
- Identify top-performing and low-performing products based on sales and quantity sold.
- Evaluate division-level revenue growth and profitability trends.
- Build an interactive sales analytics dashboard using Excel tools and DAX measures.
- Support data-driven business decisions through analytical reporting.

---

## 📊 Dashboard & Report Sections

### 👥 Customer Sales Performance
Analyzes customer-wise net sales performance across different fiscal years to identify key revenue-generating customers and sales trends.

### 🌍 Market Performance Analysis
Compares market sales performance against targets to evaluate business growth opportunities and regional performance.

### 📦 Product Performance Analysis
Provides insights into top-selling and bottom-selling products based on revenue growth and quantity sold.

### 🏢 Division Level Report
Tracks division-wise sales growth, profitability, and performance comparison between different fiscal years.

### 🆕 New Product Analysis
Identifies newly introduced products in 2021 and evaluates their contribution to total sales performance.

### 📈 Sales Growth & KPI Analysis
Monitors sales KPIs such as Net Sales, Gross Margin, Gross Margin %, and yearly growth trends through interactive reports and visualizations.

---

## 📊 Key Insights Generated
- Identified top-performing customers and markets contributing to maximum revenue.
- Analyzed year-over-year sales growth and target achievement performance.
- Evaluated Top 5 and Bottom 5 products based on quantity sold and net sales.
- Compared division-level sales performance between 2020 and 2021.
- Identified newly launched products contributing to business growth in 2021.
- Analyzed Gross Margin and profitability trends using DAX measures.
- Enabled sales performance monitoring through interactive dashboards and Pivot-based reports.

## 🧩 Data Modeling & Analytics
- Built a Star Schema data model connecting Fact and Dimension tables.
- Created relationships between Customer, Product, Market, and Sales datasets.
- Developed DAX measures for:
  - Net Sales
  - Gross Margin
  - Gross Margin %
  - Year-over-Year Growth
  - Sales vs Target Comparison
